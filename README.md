## An article I find interesting

I really like this article: [Software Engineering vs Data Science](https://medium.com/data-science/software-engineering-xor-data-science-72abbc868b8b)

What stands out to me is how the article explains that software engineering and data science are related, but not the same thing. It makes me think about how important it is to understand the difference between building tools and analyzing data. In a lot of projects, people assume that if someone is good at math or working with data, they are automatically a strong software engineer. The article pushes back on that idea and shows that each field has its own goals, skills, and ways of thinking.

I think that message is useful because it reminds me that real-world technology work is more than just one skill set. Good software needs strong engineering practices like design, testing, maintenance, and teamwork, while data science needs careful analysis and interpretation. The article makes me appreciate how much overlap there is between the two fields, but also how different they can be in practice. I like that the article is not just about technical differences, but also about how people choose their path in tech and what kinds of work they want to do.
